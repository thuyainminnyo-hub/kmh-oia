# Laphet Pilot — Community Partner Readiness & Consent Pack

**DRAFT DISCUSSION MATERIAL ONLY — NOT A CONSENT FORM, LEGAL INSTRUMENT, OR AUTHORIZATION TO COLLECT DATA.**

Pilot: KMH-LAPHET-001  
Current gate: **HOLD — Gate 0 not passed**

This pack prepares a transparent discussion with a potentially interested Laphet-producing or practicing community. It does not authorize outreach, interviews, recording, photography, recipe collection, sample collection, publication, or commercial reuse. Adapt it with the community in its preferred language and obtain appropriate local legal, ethics, data-protection and food-safety advice before use.

## 1. Non-negotiable principles

1. Participation is voluntary. A community or individual may decline without penalty or loss of services.
2. Community authorization and individual contributor consent are distinct. Obtain each where applicable.
3. The community defines what knowledge is restricted, sacred, confidential, or unsuitable for recording or publication.
4. Consent is granular. Interview permission does not imply permission for audio/video, photos, names, recipes, translation, research access, online publication, or commercial reuse.
5. No recording, collection, upload, or sampling before the relevant permissions and approvals are documented.
6. No health, probiotic, nutrition, or food-safety claims are promised. Scientific claims require evidence and competent review.
7. No payment or benefit is conditional on agreeing to publication. Agree honoraria, reimbursement, credit, or benefit-sharing transparently.
8. Participants may pause, skip questions, correct information, or withdraw. Explain practical limits after lawful public release.
9. No public release until the exact version, artifact hash, attribution, media, languages, and channels are approved by authorized community representatives and individual rights-holders.
10. Collect only the minimum necessary information. Do not collect identity documents, personal contact lists, or unrelated sensitive information.

## 3. First conversation — plain-language script

Use the participant's preferred language and invite corrections. This script is an explanation, not consent.

> KMH is exploring a small project to document one Laphet-related practice or heritage record accurately, together with the people and community who hold that knowledge. The community should decide what may be recorded and shared.
>
> Taking part is entirely your choice. You may say no, pause, skip a question, or stop. We will not record, photograph, collect recipes, take samples, or publish anything unless permission for that specific activity is agreed first.
>
> We would like to discuss what information, if any, you want documented; what must remain private or restricted; who may review it; how the community and contributors wish to be credited; which languages and channels may be used; how long information may be kept; and what benefits or costs should be agreed.
>
> An interview does not automatically permit photos, naming, translation, research sharing, public posting, or commercial reuse. Those are separate choices.
>
> We cannot promise health benefits, food-safety certification, UNESCO recognition, funding, or commercial outcomes. Scientific statements need appropriate evidence and qualified review.
>
> You may take this explanation away, discuss it with others, and request changes before deciding. Would you like to continue this discussion, with no recording or collection today?

Record questions and requested changes only with permission.

## 5. Information and rights notice

Before any consent decision, explain what information is requested and why; who can access raw recordings, notes, images, samples and drafts; storage location and security; retention and deletion; researcher/service-provider access; possible cross-border access; public copying risks; correction, withdrawal, complaint and takedown processes; and any agreed honorarium, reimbursement, credit, or community benefit.

Do not promise absolute deletion from third-party caches or copies already made by the public.

## 6. Separate authorization records

Keep separate evidence records for:

1. **Community authorization:** community-defined decision process, scope, date, authorized representatives, conditions, and review/expiry date.
2. **Individual contributor consent:** each contributor's choices for interview, voice, image, name, recipe contribution, and personal material.
3. **Producer/site permission:** responsible producer or site controller's permission for access, observation, process documentation, and samples.
4. **Research/ethics and sample permissions:** applicable institutional, legal, ethics, biosafety, laboratory, and regulatory approvals before research or sampling.
5. **Publication approval:** approval of the exact final artifact hash, wording, attribution, languages, media, and channels.

One record must never substitute for another.

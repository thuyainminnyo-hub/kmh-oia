## Claim versus evidence

The social post is a claim, not sufficient evidence by itself. This record does not yet contain a verified original event video, an official Tesla statement, technical documentation, or independent reporting that establishes the claimed demonstration occurred as described.

Do not treat earlier conversational assertions about a dated launch event, upside-down operation, flight capability, or production timing as verified until primary and reputable independent sources are attached.

## Questions to verify

- What exactly appears in the original, unedited live footage?
- Is the vehicle a physical Roadster prototype, a staged demonstration, CGI, or edited footage?
- If upside-down operation is shown, what mechanism produces and maintains adhesion or downforce?
- If lift is shown, what propulsion/lift mechanism is documented, and for how long and under what safety constraints?
- Are the claims supported by official specifications and independent testing?
- Is the demonstrated capability a prototype feature, a planned feature, or a production-ready capability?

## KMH QA rule

**Impressive demonstration ≠ independently verified production capability.**

Classify each statement as VERIFIED, PARTIALLY VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED, or CONTRADICTED. Do not mark PASS while essential evidence is missing; use PENDING VERIFICATION rather than guessing.

## KMH workflow relevance

1. Intake a public claim.
2. Preserve the original wording and source.
3. Separate factual claims from interpretation.
4. Collect primary and independent evidence.
5. Evaluate technical plausibility without substituting speculation for proof.
6. Record a traceable PASS / FAIL / PENDING decision.

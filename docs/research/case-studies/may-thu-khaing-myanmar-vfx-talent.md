## 4. Compositing role

Compositing integrates photographed plates and CG elements into a coherent final shot. Depending on the production, a compositor may work on integration, edge treatment, color/light matching, roto/paint support, cleanup, depth integration, and continuity. Public credits do not disclose which exact tasks the artist performed on each title.

## 5. Transferable industry lessons

1. Specialist roles create a clearer skills signal than a generic “video editor” or “artist” label.
2. A title credit is evidence of association with a production, not a complete portfolio or proof of shot ownership.
3. Film and television output is collaborative; individual contribution must be described precisely.
4. Global employability depends on skills, portfolio evidence, communication, delivery discipline, and production experience.
5. AI-assisted tools may accelerate selected tasks, but human artistic review and production accountability remain important.

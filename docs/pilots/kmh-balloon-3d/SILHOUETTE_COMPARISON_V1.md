# KMH_BAL_001 — Reference-to-Geometry Silhouette Comparison v1.0

## Decision
**QUALITATIVE REVIEW ONLY — PARTIAL_PASS.** Calibrated silhouette fitting and dimension residual analysis are NOT_READY. Geometry v2 remains HOLD.

## Baseline
Geometry v1 body and lower-skirt files were re-hashed before comparison. Both remain normalized arbitrary-unit meshes; no physical scale is inferred.

## What was compared
- The normalized body blockout's orthographic front/rear and side projections.
- Original semantic views: 16812, 16813, 16814.
- Supplementary video screenshots: 16826–16842.
- Separate 2019 Yan-Khwe-attached frames: 16822 and 16824.
- Ground-laid radial artwork: 16844, explicitly not used as bottom-ring dimensional evidence.

## Findings
1. The current body blockout is rotationally symmetric with circular horizontal sections. This is a model property, not a confirmed property of the real balloon.
2. The original photographs are useful for semantic artwork review, but they are not calibrated orthographic views.
3. The supplementary screenshots are video frames, not independent calibrated camera stations.
4. A qualitative silhouette plausibility review is possible; a reliable pixel-to-model dimensional residual is not.
5. Yan-Khwe remains a separate visual attachment asset and is excluded from balloon-shell fitting.
6. The lower-skirt blockout remains separate. Image 16844 documents laid-out radial artwork, not a measured bottom ring.

## Gate

## Next evidence required
A new capture set should identify each view's asset state, camera location/orientation, lens or focal length where available, scale reference, and overlapping coverage. At least front, rear, left, right, crown, and lower-opening views are needed. Record the measurement method and uncertainty for physical dimensions. Do not infer dimensions from the current perspective images.

## Scope

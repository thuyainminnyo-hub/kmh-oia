# KMH TerraLens AI

**Tagline:** See Earth. Understand Change. Act with Intelligence.

**Product category:** AI-powered Earth Observation & Land Intelligence Platform

## Product identity
KMH TerraLens AI turns satellite observations into traceable, reviewable land intelligence. It is designed to compare observations over time, surface candidate changes, preserve source provenance, and require QA and human review before a result is treated as verified.

## Initial product scope
- Product: Land Change Detection
- Pilot AOI: Taunggyi District, Shan State, Myanmar
- Primary imagery: Sentinel-2 Level-2A
- Initial analytical signal: NDVI difference screening
- Evidence standard: source scene IDs, acquisition times, processing parameters, output checksums, QA report and human review

## Product principles
1. Evidence before claims.
2. Preserve satellite source provenance.
3. Separate candidate signals from verified land-cover change.
4. Make QA failures visible; never silently relax thresholds.
5. Require human review before a result is marked verified.

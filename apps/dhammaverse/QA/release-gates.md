# DhammaVerse AI — Source, Dhamma Accuracy & Production Gates

## Gate S — Source QA
- Record canonical collection, discourse number, language, edition, stable URL and access date.
- Keep Pali source separate from each named translation.
- Compare at least two translations for material differences; do not synthesize a new “canonical” translation.
- Preserve passage-level locators and source hashes when content is ingested.
- Check copyright, licence and reuse permission before copying text, audio, images or translations into public outputs.
- A URL existing is not evidence that its contents, edition, licence or translation have been reviewed.

## Gate D — Dhamma Accuracy
- Separate canonical wording, translator rendering, commentary, editorial explanation and modern adaptation.
- Verify quotations word-for-word against the named edition.
- Do not equate a sutta with modern science or clinical psychology.
- Record doctrinal ambiguity and variant interpretations; unresolved issues remain HOLD.
- Require qualified Pali/Dhamma review and Burmese-language review before public release.
- Never fabricate Buddha quotations or attribute modern paraphrases to the Buddha.

## Gate P — Production Evidence
- Keep source-to-script-to-scene-to-audio-to-subtitle traceability.
- Maintain asset origin, licence, generation/edit history and approval.
- Test Burmese Unicode rendering, English/Burmese subtitle sync, mobile layouts, keyboard accessibility and captions/transcripts.
- Age-appropriate versions and safeguarding review for child-facing content.
- Health-related wording must be conservative; meditation is not a substitute for professional care. Provide a stop/opt-out instruction for distress.
- Record preview URL or reproducible build, test results, reviewer identity and release commit.

## Decision states
- PROMOTE: all required evidence and reviews complete.
- HOLD: source, interpretation, rights, safety, translation or review evidence incomplete.
- REJECT: demonstrably false attribution, unsafe instruction, rights violation or material distortion.

## Pilot acceptance criteria
1. Every lesson claim has a source reference or is explicitly labelled editorial/adaptation.
2. No unverified quote is presented as canonical text.
3. Burmese and English meaning reviewed independently.
4. All media rights documented.
5. Child-facing content passes safeguarding and readability review.
6. Functional and accessibility tests pass.
7. Named human reviewer signs off before public release.
